Chosen Concept & Strategic Rationale

Comprehensive justification of why Idea C outperforms alternative models

Selected Solution

Structured Doubt Triage & Scheduling Portal (Idea C)

Approved for Implementation (BridgeX)

Core Rationale: Idea C scored highest (8.65 / 10) because it addresses both sides of the university communication problem simultaneously.

For Students: Zero Silence Anxiety

Provides transparent, real-time status tracking (Pending → Seen → Queued → Resolved) with a 48-hour SLA deadline guarantee. Students know exactly when faculty will respond.

For Faculty: Protected Working Hours

Gives professors a structured, deduplicated, single-screen batched queue. Tokenized similarity matching stops duplicate inquiries, while scheduled 5-minute slots replace chaotic cabin walk-ins.

Why Alternative Concepts Were Out-Screened:

Idea A (AI Chatbot - 6.15)

Failed on C5 & C4: AI hallucinations in university coursework undermine faculty trust and grading credibility.

Idea B (Booking App - 6.15)

Failed on C3: In-person office hours collapse during peak assignment deadlines when 120+ students have questions simultaneously.

Idea D (CR Relay - 5.05)

Failed on C1 & C2: Heavy emotional burden on Class Representatives; creates communication bottleneck.

Idea E (Campus Forum - 5.90)

Failed on C2: Becomes another unstructured flood with duplicate threads and zero faculty accountability.
